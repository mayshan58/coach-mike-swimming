ABOUT COACH MIKE

Building Skills. Creating Confidence.

Swimming is more than a skill. It is an essential life skill that builds confidence, discipline, fitness and independence.

Coach Mike offers personalised swimming instruction and aquatic services designed to support learners at different stages of their swimming journey.

Whether you are learning your first strokes, improving your technique or developing your athletic performance, there is a programme for you.
