About Me

Swimming is more than a skill — it is confidence, discipline, safety, and freedom in the water. Coach Mike is committed to helping swimmers develop their abilities through personalised instruction and a supportive learning environment.


Whether you are taking your first swimming lesson, improving your performance, or developing water safety skills, every swimmer deserves guidance that supports their individual journey.
