Meet Coach Mike

Swimming is more than a skill. It is confidence, discipline, safety and personal growth.

Coach Mike offers personalised swimming instruction and aquatic training designed to help learners develop their abilities at every stage.

Whether you are learning to swim for the first time, improving your performance or building water confidence, you are welcome.

Training Location: Ocean City Water Park, Mirema, Kenya.
